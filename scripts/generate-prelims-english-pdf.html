PAPER I: GENERAL STUDIES (10 UNITS) — 100 Questions / 200 Marks
Unit 1: Concept, Culture & History of India
  • Ancient Indian Knowledge Tradition & Ideas: Bharatvarsha, Vedas, Upanishads, Aranyakas, Brahman Granthas, Shaddarshana, Smritis, Rit, Sabha-Samiti, Ganatantra, Varnashrama, Purushartha, Rina, Sanskara, Panch Mahayagya, Principle of Karma, Bodhisattva, Tirthankara.
  • Ancient & Medieval India: Salient features, major historical events, administrative, social, and economic systems.
  • Modern Indian History: National freedom struggle and major socio-religious reform movements.
  • Post-Independence: National integration, linguistic reorganization of states, and emergence of Madhya Pradesh.
Unit 2: History, Culture & Literature of Madhya Pradesh
  • Historical Dynasties of MP: Major historical milestones, ancient and medieval ruling dynasties of Madhya Pradesh.
  • Freedom Movement: Role and prominent contribution of Madhya Pradesh in India's independence movement.
  • Art & Architecture: Major arts, classical and folk architecture, sculpture, painting, and religious traditions.
  • Folk Culture & Literature: Regional folk dances, songs, theater, fairs, festivals, and major literary figures and works.
  • Tourism & Heritage: World Heritage Sites (Khajuraho, Sanchi, Bhimbetka), prominent historical, religious, and natural tourist spots.
Unit 3: Geography of India
  • Physical Features: Physiographic divisions, mountain ranges, plateaus, coastal plains, and islands.
  • Drainage System & Climate: Major river basins, monsoon mechanisms, seasons, temperature and rainfall distribution.
  • Natural Resources: Major soil types, natural vegetation, wildlife reserves, national parks, and biosphere reserves.
  • Economic Geography: Agriculture, major crops, irrigation methods, key mineral and energy resources, conventional and non-conventional sources.
  • Demographics & Transport: Population distribution, density, growth, literacy, sex ratio, and major transport corridors (Road, Rail, Waterways).
Unit 4: Geography of Madhya Pradesh
  • Physical Divisions & Climate: Plateau of Malwa, Bundelkhand, Rewa-Panna, Narmada-Son valley, Satpura-Maikal ranges; climate variations, temperature and rainfall regimes.
  • Rivers & Water Resources: Narmada, Chambal, Betwa, Son, Tapti; major multi-purpose river valley and irrigation projects.
  • Forest & Wildlife: Forest cover, protected area network, national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, and tiger reserves of MP.
  • Minerals & Energy: Coal, manganese, copper, bauxite, limestone, diamond; thermal, hydro, and renewable solar/wind energy installations.
  • Agriculture & Industries: Major kharif and rabi crops, agro-climatic zones, food processing, and key industrial clusters.

Unit 5: Constitutional System of India & Madhya Pradesh
  • Constitution of India: Constituent Assembly, Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles of State Policy, and Fundamental Duties.
  • Union Government: President, Prime Minister, Council of Ministers, Parliament, Supreme Court, and Judicial Review.
  • State Constitutional Framework (MP): Governor, Chief Minister, State Council of Ministers, Legislative Assembly, High Court of Madhya Pradesh.
  • Local Self-Government: 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts; Panchayati Raj and Urban Local Bodies in MP.
  • Good Governance: Lokayukta, State Human Rights Commission, Information Commission, and public service guarantee delivery systems.
Unit 6: Economy of India & Madhya Pradesh
  • Indian Economy: National Income, GDP growth trends, sectors of economy (Agriculture, Industry, Services), Inflation, and Budgetary process.
  • Financial Institutions: Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Commercial Banks, SEBI, Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), and Financial Inclusion.
  • External Sector: Foreign trade, Balance of Payments, foreign direct investment (FDI), and international financial institutions (IMF, World Bank, WTO).
  • Madhya Pradesh Economy: State GDP growth trends, agricultural scenario, MSMEs, industrial policy, infrastructure, and One District One Product (ODOP) initiative.
  • Social Welfare: Sustainable development initiatives, education, health, employment schemes, and women/child development welfare programs.
Unit 7: Science, Environment & Health
  • Basic Scientific Branches: Core conceptual knowledge of Physics, Chemistry, and Biology fundamentals.
  • Scientific Achievements & Space Tech: Premier Indian research institutions (ISRO, DRDO, CSIR), satellites, launch vehicles, and remote sensing applications.
  • Human Health & Nutrition: Human physiology, balanced diet, macronutrients/micronutrients, malnutrition, and deficiencies.
  • Diseases & Public Health: Infectious and non-communicable diseases; genetic disorders with special focus on Sickle Cell Anemia (causes, diagnosis, management & Mission Sickle Cell).
  • Environment & Ecology: Ecosystems, biodiversity conservation, Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), environmental pollution, climate change, and Disaster Management frameworks.
Unit 8: Current International, National & Regional Affairs
  • Current Events: Major political, economic, scientific, and geopolitical events of international and national significance.
  • State Current Affairs: Major schemes, events, policy decisions, and notable developments in Madhya Pradesh.
  • Personalities & Places: Eminent personalities in news, awards, honors, summits, and bilateral/multilateral agreements.
  • Sports & Games: Olympic, Paralympic, Commonwealth, Asian Games, National Games; sports awards (Khel Ratna, Arjuna, Dronacharya, Vikram Award); sports academies and institutions in MP.
Unit 9: Information & Communication Technology (ICT)
  • Computer & Information Tech: Architecture of computers, hardware, software, operating systems, and computer networks.
  • Advanced Digital Tech: Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, Robotics, Internet of Things (IoT), and Cloud Computing.
  • Cybersecurity: Cyber threats (malware, phishing, ransomware), cyber laws, Information Technology Act 2000, and institutional safeguards (CERT-In).
  • E-Governance & Digital India: Citizen service portals, UPI, DigiLocker, digital public infrastructure, and E-Commerce platforms.
Unit 10: Tribes of Madhya Pradesh: Heritage, Folk Culture & Literature
  • Demography & Distribution: Geographical spread of tribal communities in MP, statutory and constitutional provisions (Article 244, 5th Schedule).
  • Major Tribal Communities: Bhil, Gond, Baiga, Sahariya, Bharia, Korku, Kol; Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs) of MP.
  • Tribal Culture & Traditions: Customs, marriage systems, festivals (Bhagoria, Meghnath, Karma), traditional arts, handicrafts, and dialects.
  • Tribal Freedom Fighters: Tantia Bhil, Bhima Nayak, Birsa Munda, Shankar Shah, Raghunath Shah, Badal Bhoi, and Rani Durgavati.
  • Welfare Institutions: Tribal Research Institute, tribal museums, welfare schemes, and literature preservation initiatives.
PAPER II: GENERAL APTITUDE TEST (CSAT) — 100 Questions / 200 Marks (Qualifying 33%)
Paper II Curriculum Breakdown
  • 1. Reading Comprehension: Passages evaluating analytical understanding and deduction.
  • 2. Interpersonal Skills & Communication: Verbal and organizational communication principles.
  • 3. Logical Reasoning & Analytical Ability: Syllogisms, series, deductions, patterns, and critical reasoning.
  • 4. Decision Making & Problem Solving: Administrative and situational evaluation scenarios.
  • 5. General Mental Ability: Spatial reasoning, direction sense, blood relations, and coding-decoding.
  • 6. Basic Numeracy & Data Interpretation: Class X level numbers, ratios, percentages, charts, graphs, and data sufficiency.
  • 7. Hindi Language Comprehension Skills (Class X Level): Comprehension passages tested in Hindi without English translation.
